# Writing Bundle

Writing usually gets stuck in three places: a draft that reads machine-made, ideas that come out muddled, and good material published in only one place. This bundle hands each problem to one skill: strip the machine tone from the draft, tighten expression to one thing per sentence, then turn the same material into a spoken long-form script for video and articles.

## Skills included

- [De-AI Writing](/en/skills/de-ai-writing): Write in your voice, or strip the machine tone from a draft.
- [Speak Directly](/en/skills/zhishuo): Conclusion first, reasons after: one thing per sentence, numbers instead of adjectives.
- [Spoken Storyboard](/en/skills/koubo-storyboard): Turn research into a spoken long-form script with evidence-frame illustrations for video and X Articles.

## Download

[Download the Writing Bundle ZIP](/skills/zips/writing-bundle.zip)

The ZIP contains the full files of all three skills (each skill's SKILL.md, skill.yaml, and bundled files), with a README at the root listing the members and how to install them.

## Who it is for

People who regularly write newsletter articles, investment briefs, or spoken long-form pieces and want the finished draft to sound like themselves.

## Install

Unzip the file and place the skill folders you need into your agent's skills directory (the path differs by agent; follow your tool's documentation). Each skill's SKILL.md explains its own usage; Spoken Storyboard also ships an INSTALL-MUSE.md.
