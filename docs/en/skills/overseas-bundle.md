# Overseas Bundle

An overseas site has two stages: get traffic first, then turn traffic into paying users. This bundle hands each stage to one skill: New-Keyword Launch turns an unproven search hypothesis into a landing page on the same day and adds login and payments once traffic shows up; Monetize & Convert handles dwell time, pricing, retention, and payment instrumentation after traffic arrives.

## Skills included

- [Overseas New-Keyword Launch](/en/skills/overseas-new-keyword-launch): Turn a search hypothesis into a landing page and a post-launch SEO/GEO validation plan.
- [Overseas Monetize & Convert](/en/skills/haiwai-monetize-convert): Fake generation, anchor pricing, retention hooks, and Stripe event risk controls to lift paid conversion.

[Download the Overseas Bundle ZIP](/skills/zips/overseas-bundle.zip)

## Who it is for

People running an overseas side-project site — with a site already live or about to launch — who want to go from first traffic to first payment.

## Install

Unzip the file and place the skill folders you need into your agent's skills directory (the path differs by agent; follow your tool's documentation). Each skill's SKILL.md explains its own usage.
