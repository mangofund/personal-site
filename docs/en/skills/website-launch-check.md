## What it does

Website Launch QA is a launch checklist across nine categories: pages, mobile layout, links, forms, SEO, share cards, performance, privacy, and rollback. You run it when a site is about to go live or has just been through a big redesign, and it hands back an evidence-backed checklist — every item needs proof, not a verbal pass, and the rollback plan is written before launch, not after something breaks.

The rule behind it is simple: "works locally" is not launched. A page that renders on your machine can still fail on a phone, a share card can still be blank, a form can still drop submissions, and a deploy can still have no way back. The checklist exists to catch those before your readers do.

## When to use it — and when not to

Use it the day a site is about to go live, right after a big redesign, or after a launch that missed something visible — share cards, mobile layout, the pages people actually land on. If the last launch taught you a lesson, this is where that lesson lives.

Do not use it to build a site from zero — that is what Personal Site Build is for. This skill assumes a site already exists and asks one question: is it actually ready for strangers?
